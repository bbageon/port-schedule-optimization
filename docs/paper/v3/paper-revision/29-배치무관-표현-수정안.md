## 원칙

1. **방법(정책)**: 배치 이름을 쓰지 않는다. "오더 정보 + 야드 상태 요약을 입력으로, 시각 차이로 계산된 비용으로 학습" 한 문장을 명시한다.
2. **환경(시뮬레이터)**: 재현을 위해 구조 설정(블록 21·크레인 2대 공유·측면 인계)은 그대로 적되, "하나의 합성 구조이며 특정 터미널로 보정되지 않았다"만 말한다. "수평형을 대표한다/수직형도 검증했다"는 말은 넣지 않는다.
3. **오늘 수직 실험(YR-317-h2)**: 본문에 넣지 않는다. 답변서 리뷰어 2 항목에 "다른 동역학에서도 같은 학습 절차가 성립함(서술 사례·미보정·배치 비교 아님)" 한 줄만.
4. **남은 증거 목록**: "수평/수직 배치 평가"를 "시뮬레이터의 실측 보정"으로 바꾼다.

## 수정 1 — §3.3 비용망 (입력 계약 명시)

**현재**
> Inputs include current waiting, inventory, remaining crane work, the public expected arrival time, route difference, and announced volume near the target time; unrealised arrival and completion times and simultaneous responses from other accepting parties are excluded.

**수정**
> Inputs are order records and yard-state summaries only: current waiting, inventory, remaining crane work, the public expected arrival time, route time difference, and announced volume near the target time. No layout geometry, crane configuration, or transfer-point description enters either network; those properties act only through the gate-in, block-in, completion, and gate-out times that the simulator produces and the cost labels derived from them. Unrealised arrival and completion times and simultaneous responses from other accepting parties are excluded.

국문: 입력은 오더 기록과 야드 상태 요약뿐이다. 배치 기하·크레인 구성·인계 지점은 어느 망에도 들어가지 않으며, 시뮬레이터가 만드는 게이트 진입·블록 진입·완료·반출 시각과 그로부터 계산된 비용 라벨을 통해서만 작용한다.

## 수정 2 — §4 실험 환경 첫 문단

**현재**
> Table 1 distinguishes design choices from literature-informed values; it does not reproduce a particular terminal.

**수정**
> Table 1 distinguishes design choices from literature-informed values. The simulator instantiates one synthetic yard structure (21 blocks, two shared cranes per block, side transfer); it is not calibrated to a particular terminal, and no claim is made that it represents a specific handling system.

국문: 시뮬레이터는 하나의 합성 야드 구조(블록 21개·블록당 크레인 2대 공유·측면 인계)를 구현한다. 특정 터미널로 보정되지 않았고 특정 하역 체계를 대표한다고 주장하지 않는다.

## 수정 3 — §5.4 남은 증거 마지막 문단

**현재**
> The one-axis synthetic layout does not validate distinct horizontal and vertical handling systems. Their travel paths, transfer points, and crane-resource sharing require explicit comparison; rotating coordinates is insufficient. Hybrid layouts are excluded. Cost-weight, peak-width, and longer-run robustness also remain untested by the existing frequency sweep.

**수정**
> Because the networks consume order and yard-state records only, transferring the method to another handling system requires no layout-specific inputs; what remains unvalidated is the simulator itself. The synthetic environment has not been calibrated against terminal measurements such as quiet-day truck turn time, crane utilisation, or daily throughput, and this draft makes no claim about specific parallel or perpendicular terminals. Cost-weight, peak-width, and longer-run robustness also remain untested by the existing frequency sweep.

국문: 망이 오더·상태 기록만 쓰므로 다른 하역 체계로 옮길 때 배치 전용 입력은 필요 없다. 검증되지 않은 것은 시뮬레이터 자체다. 합성 환경은 한가한 날 턴타임·크레인 가동률·하루 처리량 같은 실측으로 보정되지 않았고, 특정 평행·직각 터미널에 대한 주장은 하지 않는다.

## 수정 4 — §6 결론

**현재**
> Remaining work includes candidate ranking, acceptance ablations, cost and horizon sensitivity, online timing, and distinct basic layouts.

**수정**
> Remaining work includes candidate ranking, acceptance ablations, cost and horizon sensitivity, online timing, and calibration of the simulated environment against terminal measurements.

## 수정 5 — 답변서 리뷰어 2 두 번째 문단·Outstanding

**현재**
> We also clarify the spatial abstraction. The current environment uses a simplified single-axis layout and does not establish a comparison between operationally distinct parallel and perpendicular terminals. Definitions of two basic layouts are a validation design, not completed structural experiments. Hybrid layouts and replication of a particular operating terminal are outside the present scope. …
> **Outstanding.** The complete independent results, parallel/perpendicular structural evaluation, and validation against operating-terminal data are not supplied by this draft.

**수정**
> We also clarify what the policy consumes. The networks receive order records and yard-state summaries only (§3.3); no layout geometry enters them, so the method needs no layout-specific inputs. We therefore do not claim validation of parallel or perpendicular terminals. As a robustness check we trained and evaluated the same procedure in a second synthetic environment with different transfer and crane dynamics; in that environment the environment-specific time-only policy was cheaper than no reallocation on all five measurement days of one seed while unfinished work increased, and the environment itself is uncalibrated. We report this only as evidence that the learning procedure adapts to different dynamics, not as a layout comparison. Replication of a particular operating terminal is outside the present scope.
> **Outstanding.** The complete independent results and calibration of the simulator against operating-terminal measurements (turn time, crane utilisation, throughput) are not supplied by this draft.
