대상 원고: [main-revised.pdf](main-revised.pdf). 두 항목의 서술 반영에 대한 초안이며 전체 답변서가 아니다.
아래 절 번호는 개정 원고를 기준으로 한다. 다른 리뷰 결과와 통합한 뒤 최종 페이지·줄 번호를 확정한다.

## Reviewer 1 — appointment constraints and rescheduling costs

**Response draft**

Thank you for highlighting the difference between terminal cost reductions and changes that carriers can execute in practice. We have clarified this distinction in the abstract, the commitment procedure (Sect. 3.2), the cost definition (Sect. 4), and the conclusion (Sect. 6). Block-capacity checks are applied, but appointment-slot quotas are unbounded in the reported experiments. The temporal candidate range does not represent verified carrier availability or a minimum notification allowance. The objective excludes waiting outside the gate, rescheduling charges, and disruption to subsequent trips.

We added appointment-quota research to Sect. 2 and connected booking availability, carrier-approved time windows, notification requirements, and cargo/vessel deadlines to the deployment discussion. These constraints would need terminal and carrier data and a feasibility layer before recommendations could be executed operationally. We describe them as future work and avoid interpreting the reported modelled terminal-cost reduction as validated operational savings.

This revision clarifies the assumptions and their implications, but it does not provide the requested experiment under realistic quotas and rescheduling costs. We have not established whether the observed benefit remains positive under those constraints or estimated a break-even rescheduling charge. We explicitly retain this limitation.

**반영 판단:** 문헌·가정·한계 설명은 원고에 반영했다. 리뷰어의 현실 제약하 추가 실증 요구는 미충족으로 남는다.

## Reviewer 3 — target users and interaction context

**Response draft**

Thank you for asking us to clarify the intended users and their role in operational decision making. We added Sect. 3.5, “Intended Users and Operational Context.” It positions the architecture as a recommendation component alongside a terminal operating system and distinguishes three roles: terminal yard planners and operations staff review and revise assignments; carrier dispatchers assess proposed time changes against transport commitments; and drivers receive confirmed instructions and report execution difficulties.

A concrete example follows a proposed later arrival through operator review, dispatcher coordination, and communication of the confirmed instruction to the driver. We explain that the human response deadline would need to be defined operationally and is not the algorithm's 60-second review interval. The new section cites research on truck waiting information at Busan New Port and Korean TOS products, together with the Busan Port Authority's description of AllCON-e, as the application context for this proposed workflow.

We also clarify that computational agreement between the proposal and acceptance networks is not human consent. The workflow has not been implemented in a TOS or AllCON-e, and no user study or field integration is claimed. The contribution addressed here is the operational decision-support architecture and its intended usage context; usability, user acceptance, and effects on human decision making remain for future evaluation.

**반영 판단:** 사용자·역할·사용 상황 설명을 추가했다. HCI(사람과 컴퓨터의 상호작용) 효과나 실제 배포를 검증했다고 답하지 않는다.
